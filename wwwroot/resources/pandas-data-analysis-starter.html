Pandas Data Analysis — Starter Guide

Essential sequence

  1. pd.read_csv() to load data.
  2. df.head(), df.shape, df.info() to inspect.
  3. Select columns and rows deliberately.
  4. Handle missing and duplicate records.
  5. Group, aggregate and merge datasets.
  6. Reshape data when the reporting question requires it.
  7. Visualize and communicate the result.
Mini-project: take a sales CSV and calculate total revenue, revenue by category, monthly trend, top products and the percentage of missing values by column.
